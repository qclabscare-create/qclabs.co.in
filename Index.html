Solar Rooftop & Power-Grid Solutions

The Most Compact and Reliable Solar Rooftop Solution

A solar rooftop system harnesses energy from the sun. It primarily consists of Solar PV Panels, which convert sunlight into DC power; a Solar Inverter, which converts DC into AC to meet household or commercial requirements; and Mounting Structures and accessories that ensure optimal performance — designed to deliver maximum output even under limited sunlight.

Powering homes & apartments, offices & commercial buildings, schools, hospitals, resorts, farms and more.

Solar Pumping Solutions

The Complete, Efficient Solar Water Pumping Solution

A solar pumping system is a pumping system driven by solar power. It has three key components: Solar PV panels that convert sunlight into DC power, a Solar Pump Controller that converts power as per the motor requirements, and the solar pump set — designed to give optimum output even with limited available solar power.

Lifts water from a well, river, lake, artificial lake or tubewell and circulates it across farms or other applications.
